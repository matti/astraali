# Research and creative decisions

The research began with the engine [README](https://github.com/jumalauta/jml-engine-webgl/blob/e58a1f6fc16e75003b2509a5471db27645191b41/README.md), [documentation](https://github.com/jumalauta/jml-engine-webgl/blob/e58a1f6fc16e75003b2509a5471db27645191b41/documentation.md), and source at **e58a1f6fc16e75003b2509a5471db27645191b41**. I read the loader, scene/player dispatch, image/shader binding, effects initialization, timer and audio implementation. The examples index was checked out at **e3290b9a71b7bec6f813569843c5b49583180741**. Revisions below are the supplied submodule revisions, not moving branch heads.

Jumalauta is a prolific demogroup, not an effect category. The [group catalogue](https://demozoo.org/groups/1297/) contains many different media and collaborations. I looked for what individual productions commit to, rather than extracting a compulsory palette, logo or visual recipe.

## Limits of audiovisual inspection

I inspected actual rendered frames at the times above and the complete decoded soundtrack spectrograms. The three browser players had running AudioContexts and playing audio sources. **I could not actually hear audio in this environment.** Observations about sonic structure are numerical/spectral and source-based, not listening judgements. The linked videos identify the productions; I did not claim to have watched those YouTube uploads. The visual contact sheet and spectra in the working research directory record the local inspections.

## Original concept

**TEMPORARY / FOREVER** treats a traffic cone with escalating ceremonial seriousness. It begins as a tiny diagram, becomes a monument, multiplies into an organization, suspends itself for paperwork, occupies the city, closes the sky and dissociates into orbiting parts. The return is an extension of the work order; the receipt says “STILL HERE.” Orange reflective material, paper, dark ink, a blue technical drawing and compressed grotesk lettering connect these transformations. The humour comes from disproportionate commitment to a mundane premise. The ending pays that premise off instead of merely stopping an effects reel.

The shared engine's documented API and general signed-distance rendering techniques were used. All demo shaders, geometry functions, plate layouts, text, camera motion and scene timing are new for this work. There are no imported production assets, logo/fist assets, copied demo utilities or generated photographic assets.
